Two digital twins learned from the same 31 days. One samples the climate every 15 minutes, the other every hour. Pick a real block of the campaign, change what the actuators did during its two treatment hours and compare what each twin says would have happened.

What the blocks measured

Every actuator group was randomised over two-hour blocks, so the campaign itself answers the question “what if this group had been at full rather than off?”. Here is that measured answer next to each twin’s, with every block simulated by a model that never saw its day.

The twins on this page are fitted on all of August so that any block can be simulated in the browser; the panel above uses the paper’s out-of-sample results (cross-validation by day). Outside weather from the greenhouse’s own station. One-step gradient-boosting models that predict the change in temperature and humidity.
